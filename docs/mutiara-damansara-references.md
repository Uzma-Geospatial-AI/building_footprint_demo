## Architect observations

[Arkitek MAA's own project portfolio](https://www.arkitekmaa.com/office) identifies Surian Tower as 26 storeys, Menara UAC as 10 storeys with a roof garden, and Menara TSR as 17 storeys / 49m above street with three basements. TSR's 49m is a published height; converting other floor counts to metres is an estimate. Nucleus/Bousteador has a separate commercial block and an open plaza, connected at first floor; curtain walls have shading devices.

The portfolio's original Surian and UAC photographs were downloaded and visually inspected. Surian shows turquoise glazing, pale spandrels, deep shade bands every several floors, a recessed vertical channel and thin crown fins. UAC shows warm taupe piers, a convex glazed central bay, projecting pale sunshades at each floor, and a columned ground frontage. These observations support simplified facade profiles, not exact window or structural dimensions.

The detached low commercial block has a dark facade and broad roof overhang. This supports a distinct profile from the adjacent blue-glazed office tower.

## Redevelopment status and known coverage gaps

- **Former eCurve / The LINES:** [Boustead's 2021 annual report](https://boustead.com.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/ar2021.pdf) records the March 2021 closure for redevelopment. The [current developer page](https://bprop.com.my/the-lines/), checked 24 October 2026, describes a future 67-storey project and explicitly labels its illustrations artist impressions. Those sources do not establish completed construction on the check date. Therefore the old 30m OSM mall volume and proposed full-height tower must not be shown as a verified current building. Record the parcel as a redevelopment gap pending current ground evidence. Publication dates and retrieval dates are not interchangeable.
- **Surian Residences:** acquired map data does not provide sufficiently resolved separate tower ground footprints. Do not turn a residential parcel/site envelope into a tower, nor georeference an oblique roof photograph as a ground survey. Retain this as an explicit coverage gap until defensible tower footprints are available. The architect's former project URL currently displays a maintenance page, so it cannot establish exact current dimensions.
- **Reflection Residences:** no defensible tower footprint was identified in the acquired source inventory. Retain an explicit gap; a point label alone is insufficient to invent a building polygon.
- **Private residential compounds:** cadastral lots, residential landuse outlines and aerial roof outlines are not automatically building ground footprints. Mapped houses may be reconstructed; missing houses must remain tracked rather than filled with invented rows.
- **Interior plans:** the [KidZania operator city map](https://www.kidzania.com.my/map) and Curve retail directory are public navigation diagrams. They do not establish surveyed strata boundaries, load-bearing structure or emergency-route certification and are not new measured interior models.

## Terrain and accessibility limits

Use independent credible road/frontage datums and the existing limited terrain adjustment policy. Parking basements, elevated footbridges, station roofs and a building's solid ground contact are different geometric parts. Open access routes must remain open. Photo-supported facade profiles can be simplified, but should not create generic thick soil platforms or whole-building floating gaps to avoid a road collision. Keep local height assumptions and unsupported facade dimensions visible in model metadata.
